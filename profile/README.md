## Soffi Originals
A small, invite-only group of designers, PMs, and builders pushing Soffi to its limits and shaping what it becomes.

Every repo here is a live testbed. What you see on `main` is whatever the group shipped last. No staging, no polish pass, just real work in motion. Public to read and fork. Editing is by invitation.

### The demos

- **[finance-demo](https://github.com/Soffi-Originals/finance-demo)** is Northwind, a personal finance dashboard with balance, spending categories, and recent transactions.
- **[habit-demo](https://github.com/Soffi-Originals/habit-demo)** is Cadence, a journal-style habit tracker with a month-long completion heatmap and warm serif typography.

More on the way. The Originals are just getting started.
